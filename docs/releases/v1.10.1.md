# Meshcopia 1.10.1 — Accurate ZIP download sizes

Catalog cards display the exact bytes of their linked ZIP using readable B/KB/MB units. Small multiple selections no longer round down to 0.0 MB. Before preparing a combined ZIP the selection shows its estimated complete STORE ZIP size; after preparation it shows the actual Blob size. Removing or clearing selections invalidates stale totals. Downloaded payloads, package identities and historical archive hashes are unchanged.

Verification includes canonical full-site checks, original viewers, all173 card sizes, and desktop/mobile real downloads of the reported small-model selection.
