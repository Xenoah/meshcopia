# Meshcopia 1.4.0

12 new original reusable street models, bringing the public collection to 83 models and 36 textures (119 assets).

- Street access: removable bollard, cycle rack, pedestrian railing, curb ramp, speed cushion, water-fill road barrier.
- Civic fixtures: litter bin, three-stream recycling station, dual-height drinking fountain, parcel locker bank, parking meter, two-outlet hydrant.
- Self-contained GLB, untextured Y-up meter-scale STL alternate, honest exact-mesh preview, named parts, structured creation provenance, and CC BY 4.0 attribution.
- Adaptive detail reduces invisible tessellation while preserving useful silhouette and hardware. Bollard: 4,828 triangles; speed cushion: 3,660.
- Six infrastructure drafts are intentionally excluded pending their publication milestone. No draft is included in public catalog/download bundles.

Original agent-authored procedural geometry, no third-party meshes/scans/brands. Static games/visualization only; not validated for manufacturing, 3D printing, accessibility or safety-critical equipment. Per-component UVs are not a packed lightmap atlas. STL has no color/UV/material information.
